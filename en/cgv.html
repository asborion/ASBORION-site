Every project starts with a free, detailed quote: pages, features, add-ons, timeline and price. Quotes are valid for 30 days. The order is confirmed once the signed quote and the deposit have been received. Anything not listed in the quote requires an additional quote.

Prices are in euros. VAT not applicable, article 293 B of the French Tax Code (CGI). The “from” prices shown on this website are indicative: only the price in the signed quote is binding.

  • 30% deposit when the quote is signed.

Late payments incur penalties at three times the French statutory interest rate. Business clients are also charged a fixed €40 recovery fee. ASBORION may postpone the launch or suspend maintenance until payment is received in full.

4. Process and timeline

Timelines shown on this website and in quotes are estimates. They start once the deposit and all required content (text, photos, logo, access) have been received. The client agrees to provide this content and to reply to approval requests within a reasonable time: any delay on their side pushes back delivery accordingly. If the client does not respond for 30 days, the project may be paused and rescheduled based on availability.

5. Revisions

The price includes 2 rounds of revisions on the design and 1 round after development. A round covers all feedback sent at once. Beyond that, or for requests that change something already approved, work is charged at €45 per hour, with the client's agreement.

6. Delivery and approval

Once development is complete, the website is presented to the client for approval. If the client raises no issues within 15 days of delivery, the website is considered approved. The website goes live once the balance has been paid.

7. Maintenance

Maintenance plans are monthly, with no commitment.

  • Standard (€40 / month): hosting, security updates, backups.
  • Plus (€80 / month): Standard + 1 hour of content updates per month.

Content updates included in the Plus plan cover replacing or adding text, photos, prices, opening hours or news on existing pages. They do not include new pages or features, redesigning a section or design changes. These requests are quoted separately.

Unused hours don't carry over to the next month. Requests are sent by email, grouped where possible, and handled within 5 business days. Time spent beyond the included hour is charged at €45 per hour, only with the client's agreement. ASBORION may consider that a request goes beyond maintenance and offer a quote instead.

Maintenance is paid monthly, at the start of each month. It can be cancelled at any time by email; cancellation takes effect at the end of the current month. The client then receives a copy of their website files.
